About Us

One team, one requisition

Mideast Marine Services LLC is a UAE-based ship supply and marine services company serving vessels at UAE ports. We quote to IMPA codes, arrange transport and customs clearance, and deliver alongside with our own fleet of delivery trucks.

Provisions are supplied from our own inventory at 12 hours' notice, technical stores in 6 hours, and approved accounts enjoy 30 days credit.

Our Services

Complete ship supply & marine services

🥬

Provisions

Fresh vegetables and fruit; meat, poultry, fish and seafood; dairy, eggs and dry provisions; bonded-style stores to order. From our own inventory at 12 hrs notice.

🧰

Deck, Engine & Cabin Stores

IMPA-coded quotation lines for linen, galley, stationery, cleaning, electrical and safety items. Technical stores in 6 hours.

Spares & Fabrication

Engine, pump and turbocharger spares, anchor chains, fenders, pipes and fittings, SS316 reducers and flanges, sourced or made to drawing, sample or specification.

🧭

Nautical Charts & Publications

Navigational charts and flags, Admiralty Sailing Directions, List of Lights and routeing guides.

Vessel Automation Repair

Repair of vessel automation, alarm & monitoring systems and control boards (PCB repair), plus PLCs, sensors and transmitters. Fault-finding at berth or anchorage.

🛟

LSA & FFA Inspection

Annual inspection and servicing of Life-Saving Appliances (LSA) and Fire-Fighting Appliances (FFA), arranged through approved third-party service stations.

🤿

Underwater Hull Cleaning

Hull cleaning arranged through trusted third-party contractors at UAE ports.

Cargo Hold Cleaning

Cargo hold cleaning arranged through trusted third-party contractors.
